Having any issues? Find missing steps here.


- Step 1: Purchasing the package

1. Sign in to your account using Google.
2. Find the product you want to purchase and add it to your cart.
3. Navigate to the Cart tab to review your selected products.
4. Enter your required credentials and proceed with the checkout process.
5. Once your purchase is complete, navigate to My Products to find and access your newly purchased product.

- Step 2: Installing the file

1. Navigate to My Products and download the .zip file.
2. Once the download is complete, locate the file in your browser's download history or designated download folder.
3. Open the file's destination folder and extract the contents of the .zip file.
4. Once extracted, you can access and use your MIDI and WAV files.

Getting the instruments to work

- Getting the Decent Sampler instruments to work

1.Download Decent Sampler from here.
2.Navigate to the Instruments folder inside the folder you downloaded from us.
3.Locate the .dspreset file and open it using Decent Sampler.
4.Your instrument should now be loaded and ready to use!

How to Install and Use Serum 2 Presets

1. Purchase and install Serum 2 from here.
(Please note that Serum 2 is paid software and requires a valid license.)
2. Navigate to the Presets folder inside the folder you downloaded from us.
3. Locate the Serum 2 preset files and import them into Serum 2.
4. Your presets should now be available to use in Serum 2!
